Using Koinonia with care

Last updated 17 August 2026

These Terms set the basic expectations for using Koinonia within a church or Christian community. Koinonia has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users or behavior.

Community access

Access is tied to a community membership and role. Administrators may change roles, disable access or remove membership where authorized to protect the community or operate the service.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abuse

Only authorized publishers and administrators can create posts. You retain responsibility for content you submit and confirm that you have permission to share it. Reporting and blocking

Members can report an objectionable post from the post’s menu, report a user from that user’s profile, and block a user so that user’s posts no longer appear. Reports are shared with authorized community administrators without exposing the reporter to the reported user.

Enforcement

Administrators may dismiss reports after review, remove objectionable or violating content, suspend access, or terminate access or an account. These actions may be taken for objectionable content, harassment, threats, abuse, unlawful content, other violations of these Terms or the Community Standards, or serious or repeated misconduct.
