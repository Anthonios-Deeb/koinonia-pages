Account deletion

Delete your Koinonia account

You can delete your account in the app. If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by email without reinstalling it.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Koinonia.
  2. Open Profile → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

Request deletion outside the app

If you cannot sign in or no longer have Koinonia installed, use the email link below. Koinonia will verify account ownership through a safe process. Never send a password, one-time verification code, or authentication token by email.

Start deletion request

External deletion requests are completed within 30 days after the request and required ownership verification are received, unless a legal obligation requires limited retention.

What deletion removes

  • The authentication account and active sessions.
  • Profiles, community memberships, saved posts, and push tokens linked to the account.
  • Posts and relational media records authored by the account.
  • Block relationships and other account-linked records that are configured to be removed with the account.

Known uploaded post media and profile photos are submitted for Storage deletion immediately. If a Storage deletion fails, a residual object may remain until operational cleanup is completed.

Limited retention

Deletion does not create a permanent phone-number re-registration block. The same phone number may register a new account after a normal user-initiated deletion. Administrative restrictions for disabled, removed, or banned users remain in force.

  • Enrollment security records, including protected phone or network-address digests and attempt timestamps, may be retained as needed for rate limiting, security, and abuse prevention.
  • Moderation and safety evidence, including reports, target snapshots, decisions, and audit events, may be retained as needed for safety, accountability, and legal obligations. Account identifiers are removed from retained audit references where applicable.
  • Managed backups may contain copies until they expire under the hosting provider’s backup lifecycle.

These limited records do not preserve an active profile or restore community access. Koinonia does not publish a fixed retention period where the current system does not technically enforce one.
