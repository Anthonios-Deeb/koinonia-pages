Community Standards

A community shaped by dignity and care

Effective August 9, 2026 · Published by Anthonios Deeb

Koinonia is a shared Christian community space. These standards apply to posts, profiles, media, and other content available through the app.

Treat people with dignity

Do not harass, bully, threaten, demean, or discriminate against another person or group. Disagreement must remain respectful.

Keep the community safe

Do not share sexual content, graphic violence, credible threats, instructions for wrongdoing, or content that places another person at risk.

Protect privacy

Do not disclose private contact details, health information, prayer details, photographs, or other personal information without appropriate permission.

Be truthful and relevant

Do not impersonate others, deliberately spread misleading information, spam the community, or use Koinonia for unrelated promotion.

Respect ownership

Only share text, images, video, audio, and livestream links that you created or have permission to use.

Reporting and enforcement

Use the in-app Report post or Report user action when content may violate these standards. You can also block a user whose posts you do not want to see. Administrators review reports and may dismiss them, remove posts, or disable accounts. Do not misuse reporting to target or intimidate others.

Contact

Safety contact: Koinonia.support@gmail.com. Reports raised in the app are reviewed by community administrators. Urgent safety concerns sent to this address are acknowledged within 48 hours. Koinonia is not an emergency service; contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.
