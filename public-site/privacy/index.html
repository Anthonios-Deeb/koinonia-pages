Privacy Policy

How Koinonia handles information

Effective August 9, 2026 · Data controller: Anthonios Deeb

Koinonia uses information needed to provide secure phone sign-in, community profiles, posts and media, saved posts, notifications, moderation, and account administration.

Information collected

  • Verified mobile phone number and account identifier.
  • First and last name, community membership, role, and account status.
  • An optional profile photo.
  • For authorized publishers: post titles, text, images, videos, audio, and supported YouTube links.
  • Saved-post records.
  • If notifications are enabled, a device push token and the device name reported by the operating system. An OS-reported device name may contain a person’s chosen name, such as “Sarah’s iPhone.”
  • Security records such as sign-in attempts, timestamps, and protected phone or network-address digests used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics only when production diagnostics are enabled.

Community membership and prayer or faith content may reveal religious beliefs. Koinonia treats that information as sensitive.

How information is used

  • Authenticate accounts and manage community access.
  • Display profiles, posts, media, and saved content.
  • Deliver optional post notifications.
  • Receive reports, apply blocks, moderate content, and maintain safety records.
  • Protect the service against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose reliability problems when diagnostics are enabled.

Visibility inside a community

Names and optional profile photos can be visible to members of the same community. Posts and attached media are visible to active members unless a member blocks the author. Community administrators retain the visibility needed for moderation.

Service providers

Koinonia uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; Twilio for SMS verification; Expo Push Service, Apple Push Notification service, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for optional notifications; YouTube for embedded playback; and YouVersion for verse content. Sentry is used only when enabled in a production release.

Retention

  • Active accounts. Account, profile, membership, content, and related service data are retained while the account exists. Signing out ends the device session and disables that device’s push token, but does not delete the account or its other data.
  • Account deletion. Deletion removes account-linked data from active systems, including the authentication account, profiles, memberships, saved posts, push tokens, posts, and relational media records. Known uploaded media and profile-photo objects are submitted for deletion immediately. If a Storage deletion fails, a residual object may remain until operational cleanup is completed.
  • Enrollment security records. Protected phone and network-address digests and attempt timestamps may be retained as needed for rate limiting, security, and abuse prevention. These records are not an active profile and do not permanently prevent ordinary re-registration after user-initiated deletion.
  • Moderation and safety evidence. Reports, target snapshots, moderation decisions, and audit records may be retained as needed for safety, accountability, and legal obligations. Where applicable, a deleted account’s actor or subject identifier is removed while the audit event remains.
  • Backups. Managed backups may contain copies until they expire under the hosting provider’s backup lifecycle.

Koinonia does not publish a fixed retention period where the current system does not technically enforce one.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app from Profile → Account → Delete Account, or start a request on the account deletion page without installing the app. A normal user-initiated deletion does not permanently prevent the same phone number from registering again. Administrative restrictions for disabled, removed, or banned users remain in force.

Children

Koinonia is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights and contact

To ask what is held about you, request a correction, request deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact Koinonia.support@gmail.com. Koinonia is published and controlled by Anthonios Deeb.
