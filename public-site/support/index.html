Support

How can we help?

Contact Koinonia support for sign-in, account, privacy, safety, accessibility, or technical questions.

Email support

Support address: Koinonia.support@gmail.com. The normal first-response target is within 3 business days.

Include

  • A short description of what happened.
  • Your device platform and app version.
  • The approximate date and time.
  • Whether the issue affects sign-in, a post, a report, blocking, or account deletion.

Do not include

Never send passwords, one-time verification codes, authentication tokens, or sensitive prayer details. Support may ask for limited verification through an approved process.

Safety concerns

Use the in-app report action when possible: open a post’s menu and choose Report post, or open a member’s profile and choose Report user. You can also block a member so their posts no longer appear. For an urgent safety concern, email Koinonia.support@gmail.com with “Safety concern” in the subject. The urgent safety acknowledgement target is within 48 hours. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger; Koinonia is not an emergency service.

Account deletion

To delete your account outside the app, use the account deletion page.
